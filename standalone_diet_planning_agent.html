Food & Nutrients Guide

Simple breakdown of what essential nutrients and foods are needed for weight gain or weight loss.

Foods & Nutrients for Weight Loss
  • 🥩 Lean Protein: Keeps you full longer and preserves muscle mass.
    Foods: Egg whites, Chicken breast, Fish, Tofu, Moong Dal, Low-fat Paneer.
  • 🥬 High Fiber Vegetables & Grains: Low calorie, high volume foods that aid digestion.
    Foods: Spinach, Broccoli, Oats, Quinoa, Brown Rice, Cucumber.
  • 💧 Hydration: Essential for flushing toxins and keeping metabolism active.
    Sources: Water (3+ Liters/day), Green Tea, Black Coffee (no sugar).
Foods & Nutrients for Weight Gain
  • 🥜 Calorie-Dense Healthy Fats: Concentrated energy sources to easily build a calorie surplus.
    Foods: Peanut Butter, Almonds, Walnuts, Avocados, Whole Milk, Ghee.
  • 🍳 Muscle Building Protein: Builds new muscle tissue when paired with exercise.
    Foods: Whole Eggs, Fish, Chicken, Paneer Tikka, Soya Chunks, Whey.
  • 🍌 Energy Rich Complex Carbs: Restores glycogen to power strength workouts.
    Foods: Bananas, Sweet Potatoes, Whole Wheat Rotis, White/Brown Rice, Dates.
